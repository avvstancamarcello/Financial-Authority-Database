Warning: the fake London lawyer scam

Were you scammed with trading, crypto or a fake bank? Now a "London lawyer" contacts you and says:

"We have found your capital. To release it you must pay taxes, legal fees and recovery commissions. Pay now or you will lose it."

This is the second scam. The bait is the recovery of your stolen capital. The aim is to make you pay yet another advance.

Before paying, carry out the Effective Verification in 2 minutes.

UK Effective Verification – 3 steps

  1. A – Lawyers: Ask for their full name, firm name, physical address in London, an email on the firm's own domain, and their SRA or Bar registration number.
  2. M – Worldwide: Do they claim to practise in England and Wales? Then they must appear in one of the two official registers below. If they say "London" but are not listed, that is a red flag.
  3. E + V – Effective Verification: Search for the name yourself using the official links. Do not trust screenshots, PDFs or ID cards they send you. Only the official register counts.

Official verification – direct links to UK sources

Click and search for the name. If it is not listed, do not pay.

1. Solicitors – SRA – Solicitors Regulation Authority

The solicitor is the lawyer who handles the case and speaks to the client. This is the most common type in fund recovery cases.

Check here:
SRA Register – Search for a Solicitor / Firm →

What to check: exact name, firm name, address, email, and the status "Authorised and regulated". If the firm is not there, or the email is Gmail / Proton / Outlook, stop.

2. Barristers – Bar Standards Board

The barrister represents clients in court. If the self-styled lawyer says "I am a barrister", they must appear here.

Check here:
Bar Standards Board – Barristers' Register →

3. Law Society – Find a Solicitor

An additional register for cross-checking the data.

Check here:
Law Society – Find a Solicitor →

5 red flags of the fake London lawyer

  • Asks for an advance to release funds: taxes, fees or recovery commissions to be paid in crypto, by transfer to a foreign account or by card.
  • Uses WhatsApp / Telegram / Gmail: a genuine London firm uses its own domain and a verifiable landline.
  • Fake address: "London", but on Google Maps it is a virtual office, a coworking space or an address that does not match the firm.
  • Not listed with the SRA or the Bar: this is the decisive sign. If they are not listed, they are not an authorised lawyer in the UK.
  • Urgency and threats: "If you do not pay today you lose everything." Urgency is the technique used to stop you from checking.

What to do if the lawyer is not listed

  1. Pay nothing. Do not send identity documents and do not sign any engagement letter.
  2. Keep everything: emails, messages, phone number, and the IBAN / wallet they gave you to pay.
  3. Report it: in the UK to Action Fraud UK → and, if you are in Italy, to the Polizia Postale. Bring this page with you as a record of the check you made.
  4. Check the original intermediary: go back to the AMEV Financial Authority Database and check whether the platform that first scammed you was authorised. Also read the AMEV Equivalence page to understand the method.

AMEV methodological note

AMEV – Avvocati nel Mondo Effettiva Verifica (Lawyers Worldwide Effective Verification) – is the information brand of www.amevfirenze.it.
