Warning: the fake US lawyer scam

Were you scammed with trading, crypto or a fake bank? Now a "New York lawyer" or a "California lawyer" contacts you and says:

"We have found your capital in the USA. We have opened a federal case. To release it you must pay taxes, legal fees, a security deposit, FBI / SEC fees. Pay now or the case will be closed."

This is the second scam. The bait is the recovery of your stolen capital. The aim is to make you pay yet another advance. The words "New York", "FBI", "SEC", "federal case" are used to give an impression of power.

Before paying, carry out the Effective Verification in 2 minutes.

US Effective Verification - 3 steps

  1. A - Lawyers: Ask for their full name, firm name, physical address in the USA, an email on the firm's own domain, State of license, and bar number.
  2. M - Worldwide: Which State do they claim to be licensed in? There is no national license in the USA. Every lawyer is licensed State by State, by a state agency. If they say only "USA" without a State, that is already a red flag. If they say "New York and California", you must check both States.
  3. E + V - Effective Verification: Search for their name and bar number yourself in the State Bar of that State, starting from the ABA index below. Do not trust screenshots, PDFs or ID cards they send you. Only the State register counts. If the bar number belongs to a different name, stop: impostors often steal the bar numbers of real lawyers.

5 red flags of the fake US lawyer

  • They ask for an advance to release funds: FBI taxes, SEC fees, a federal deposit, court costs to be paid in crypto, by foreign transfer or by card.
  • They cite the FBI / SEC / a federal case: the FBI and the SEC do not release accounts on behalf of private lawyers and do not ask for money through a lawyer on WhatsApp.
  • They say only "USA" without a State: a real US lawyer can tell you which State they are licensed in and their bar number. If they cannot, stop.
  • They are not listed in the State Bar, or appear as inactive / suspended / disbarred: this is the decisive sign. Only active lawyers can practice in that State.
  • Someone else's bar number: impostors steal the bar numbers of real lawyers. Compare the name, firm and address in the register with those they gave you. If they do not match, it is not them.

What to do if they are not listed

  1. Pay nothing. Do not send identity documents, do not sign any engagement letter, do not give your IBAN / wallet.
  2. Keep everything: emails, messages, phone number, and the IBAN / wallet they gave you to pay.
  3. Report it: in the USA to the State Bar of the State they claim to belong to and to the FTC - Report Fraud →, and in Italy to the Polizia Postale. Bring this page with you as a record of the check you made.
  4. Check the original intermediary: go back to the AMEV Financial Authority Database and check whether the platform that first scammed you was authorized in the USA by the SEC / CFTC. Also read the AMEV Equivalence page to understand the method.
