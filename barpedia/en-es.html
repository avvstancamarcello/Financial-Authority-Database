Warning: the fake Spanish lawyer scam

Were you scammed with trading, crypto or a fake bank? Now a "lawyer from Madrid" or "from Barcelona" contacts you and says:

"We have located your capital in Spain. To start the recovery you must pay legal fees, court taxes and a security deposit. Pay now or the proceedings will be closed."

This is the second scam. The bait is the recovery of your stolen capital. The aim is to make you pay yet another advance.

Before paying, carry out the Effective Verification in 2 minutes.

ES Effective Verification – 3 steps

  1. A – Lawyers: Ask for their full first name and surname, firm name, physical address in Spain, an email on the firm's own domain, and their colegiación number.
  2. M – Worldwide: Do they say they operate in Madrid? Then they must appear as colegiado in Madrid. Barcelona? They must appear in Barcelona. In Spain registration is provincial, with the Colegio de la Abogacía of that province.
  3. E + V – Effective Verification: Search for the name and number yourself in the official Censo below. Do not trust screenshots, PDFs or ID cards they send you. Only the official Censo counts.

Official verification – direct links to Spanish sources

Click and search for the name. If they do not appear as colegiado ejerciente, do not pay.

1. Censo General de Letrados – Abogacía Española – National source

The single point for checking any lawyer in Spain. It gives you access to the professional data of any colegiado across the national territory, without having to call the Colegio. You can search by first name and surname or by colegiación number.

Check here:
Censo General de Letrados - Abogacía Española →

What to check: exact name, Colegio of membership, colegiación number, status "ejerciente". If they appear as "no ejerciente" or do not appear at all, they cannot act for you.

2. Madrid – Ilustre Colegio de la Abogacía de Madrid – ICAM

In Spain every practising lawyer must have a colegiado number issued by the Colegio of the province. In Madrid it is the ICAM. You can check the number or the name in the search tool on the ICAM website.

Check here:
ICAM - Ilustre Colegio de la Abogacía de Madrid →

3. Barcelona – Il·lustre Col·legi de l'Advocacia de Barcelona – ICAB

Before hiring a lawyer in Barcelona, you must check the colegiado number with the ICAB and that the professional is ejerciente.

5 red flags of the fake Spanish lawyer

  • Asks for an advance to start the recovery: security deposit, court taxes, court costs to be paid in crypto, by foreign transfer or by card.
  • Uses WhatsApp / Telegram / Gmail: a genuine Spanish firm uses its own domain, a verifiable Spanish landline and a physical address.
  • Cannot tell you their colegiación number: a genuine Spanish lawyer knows their number and their Colegio. If they hide it or make one up, stop.
  • Not listed in the Censo General de Letrados: this is the decisive sign. If they do not appear as colegiado ejerciente, they are not an authorised lawyer in Spain.
  • Urgency and threats: "If you do not pay today you lose the proceedings." Urgency is the technique used to stop you from checking.

What to do if the lawyer is not listed

  1. Pay nothing. Do not send identity documents, do not sign any engagement letter, do not give your IBAN / wallet.
  2. Keep everything: emails, messages, phone number, and the IBAN / wallet they gave you to pay.
  3. Report it: in Spain at Policía Nacional - Denuncia → and, in Italy, to the Polizia Postale. Bring this page with you as a record of the check you made. You can also tell the Colegio that the fake lawyer claims to belong to.
  4. Check the original intermediary: go back to the AMEV Financial Authority Database and check whether the platform that first scammed you was authorised in Spain by the CNMV. Also read the AMEV Equivalence page to understand the method.
