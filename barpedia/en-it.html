Warning: the fake Italian lawyer scam

Were you scammed with trading, crypto or a fake bank? Now an "Italian lawyer" contacts you and says:

"I am Avv. Rossi from Rome / Milan. I saw your report / I have found your money. To start the recovery you must pay legal fees, taxes, a security deposit. Pay now or you will lose everything."

This is the second scam. The bait is the recovery of your stolen capital. The aim is to make you pay yet another advance. In Italy it is the most dangerous one, because it speaks your language, knows your city and uses an Italian name.

Before paying, carry out the Effective Verification in 2 minutes. In Italy the verification is double.

IT Effective Verification - 4 steps, not 3

  1. A - Lawyers: Ask for their full first name and surname, the Bar Association they belong to, for example Rome, Milan, Florence, their Register registration number, and their PEC.
  2. M - Worldwide: Do they say they work in Rome? Then they must be registered with the Bar Association of Rome. Do they say Milan? They must be listed in Milan. In Italy every lawyer is registered in a single local Register.
  3. E - Effective: Search for their first and last name yourself in the Single Register below. If they are not listed, they are not a lawyer registered in Italy.
  4. V - Verification of contact details - The step that saves you: If the name is listed, compare the email, PEC and telephone number they gave you with those published in the Register. Scammers often use the real names of real lawyers. If the contact details do not match, it is not them. Contact the real lawyer only through the details in the Register, never through the details given to you by the self-styled lawyer.

Art. 37 - Prohibition on soliciting clients - Why they cannot contact you out of the blue

Italian Code of Legal Ethics, Art. 37, paragraph 5: "A lawyer is also prohibited from offering, without being asked, a personalised service, that is, one aimed at a specific person for a specific matter."
The prohibition concerns spontaneous offers. A lawyer may contact you if you are already their client, or if they write on behalf of another party, for example as the lawyer of a counterparty. In no case will they propose that you become their client to recover sums lost in online investments.

5 red flags of the fake Italian lawyer

  • They contact you out of the blue: you never sought them out. The Italian Code of Legal Ethics prohibits a lawyer from offering you, without your request, a personalised service for a specific matter. If they contact you first to offer you recovery, that is already a red flag.
  • They ask for an advance to start the recovery: security deposit, taxes, legal fees, release of funds, to be paid by transfer, crypto or card.
  • They use WhatsApp / Telegram / Gmail: a real Italian lawyer uses a PEC, the firm's domain, a verifiable landline and a physical address.
  • The name is listed but the contact details do not match: this is the most common trick in Italy. The name is real, but the email and telephone belong to the scammer. Always compare with the Register, not with what they send you.
  • Urgency and threats: "If you do not pay today you lose the case / the bank confiscates everything." Urgency is the technique used to stop you from checking.

What to do if they are not listed, or if the contact details do not match

  1. Pay nothing. Do not send identity documents, do not sign any engagement letter, do not give your IBAN / wallet.
  2. Keep everything: emails, PEC, messages, phone number, and the IBAN / wallet they gave you to pay.
  3. Report it: to the Polizia Postale and to the Bar Council that the fake lawyer claims to belong to. If they used the name of a real lawyer, also warn the real lawyer through the details in the Register: they may not know that their name is being used.
  4. Check the original intermediary: go back to the AMEV Financial Authority Database and check whether the platform that first scammed you was authorised in Italy by CONSOB / Banca d'Italia. Also read the AMEV Equivalence page to understand the method.
