Financial Conduct Authority – United Kingdom

Institutional Overview

The Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) is the United Kingdom’s independent regulatory body responsible for supervising financial markets, investment firms, brokers, banks, and consumer protection frameworks. It operates under the Financial Services and Markets Act and ensures integrity, transparency, and stability across UK financial operations.

Market Supervision

The FCA supervises investment firms, forex brokers, crypto asset providers, insurance intermediaries, and banking entities. It enforces strict licensing requirements and conducts ongoing monitoring to prevent fraud, market abuse, and unauthorized financial activities.
