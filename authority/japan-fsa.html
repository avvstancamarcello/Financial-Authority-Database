Japan · Financial Services Agency (FSA) orbital guide

Official resources: FSA
National financial authority: Financial Services Agency (FSA) of Japan
Official website: www.fsa.go.jp/en
Scope: The FSA oversees banks, insurance companies, financial instruments business operators, crypto-asset exchange service providers and other regulated financial businesses in Japan. Criminal complaints belong to the police; market-abuse reports can be submitted to SESC.

Independent orbital guide
This AMEV page is not published, certified or endorsed by the FSA. It is not investment advice. Official links are provided for information, not as a guarantee of safety or recovery.

A. Consumer protection and fraud precautions

  • FSA · Homepage and alerts — fsa.go.jp/en
    Start with the official website and consult original notices when assessing messages presented as FSA communications.
  • FSA · Watch out for suspicious investment solicitation — fsa.go.jp/ordinary/chuui/attention.html
    Precautions against solicitation by unregistered operators. Exercise extreme caution and avoid transactions with unregistered operators.
  • FSA · Newsletter — fsa.go.jp/en/newsletter
    FSA news and periodic notices.

B. Warnings against unregistered / unauthorised firms

  • FSA · “Cold Calling” – Investors Alert — fsa.go.jp/en/regulated/cold-calling
    Warnings concerning unauthorised entities and fictitious government bodies. An entity's absence from a list does not mean an offer is authorised.
  • FSA · Crypto-asset information and warnings — fsa.go.jp/policy/virtual_currency
    Consult the current official information and warnings on unregistered crypto-asset exchange operators. Unregistered operators may leave customers exposed to blocked withdrawals, excessive fees or loss of contact.
  • IOSCO · I-SCAN — iosco.org/i-scan
    International portal of securities regulators' warnings; useful when a firm claims a licence in another jurisdiction.

C. Registration checks – search authorised firms

  • FSA · List of licensed (registered) financial institutions — fsa.go.jp/en/regulated/licensed
    Sector-by-sector lists of banks, financial instruments business operators, insurers and other regulated firms. Compare the firm's claimed registration and permitted activity with the relevant official list.
  • FSA · Financial business operator collective search — search.fsa.go.jp
    Search across sectors by name or telephone number. A missing result is a warning sign, not a definitive finding; check the relevant register and contact the authority. Registration does not mean the FSA approves an investment or guarantees its safety.

D. Reporting fraud – SESC and FSA Counseling Office

  • SESC · Securities Watch & Report — fsa.go.jp/sesc/watch
    For information on market manipulation, insider trading and other securities-market misconduct. SESC does not recover funds or provide individual legal advice. Check its official page for current contact details.
  • FSA · Counseling Office for Financial Services Users — fsa.go.jp/en/contact.html
    For general questions and consultations about financial services. Telephone: 0570-016811 (IP phone: 03-5251-6811), weekdays 10:00–17:00 Japan time. General enquiries: equestion@fsa.go.jp. Check current contact details and service limitations on the official site; the office does not recover money.

E. How to use this guide – 60-second check

  1. Copy the exact firm name and telephone number shown in the offer.
  2. Search in C (collective search / licensed lists), including the relevant activity and contact details.
  3. Search in B (cold calling / crypto warnings).
  4. If the firm appears in a warning or its details do not match, stop and do not pay.
  5. Absence from a list does not mean an offer is safe. Contact SESC or the Counseling Office as appropriate and, for criminal matters, the police in your country.

Keep the firm name, website, telephone, claimed registration number, screenshots, transfer receipts and chat logs. Do not send passwords, seed phrases or remote-access codes to any “recovery” contact.
